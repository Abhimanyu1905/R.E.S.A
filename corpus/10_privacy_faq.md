## What personal data does R.E.S.A Support collect during the shopping experience?
We collect personal identity data (full legal name, email address, physical delivery address, contact telephone number), financial transaction metadata (card network brand, last 4 digits of payment card, tokenized transaction identifiers, billing postal code), and technical device telemetry (IPv4/IPv6 address, operating system build, browser user agent string, session cookie identifiers, referring campaign URLs). This information is gathered solely to complete commercial orders, prevent transactional fraud, and optimize checkout navigation.

## How long is customer data retained in active databases?
Data retention schedules follow strict regulatory retention schedules:
- Web server access logs, session tokens, and clickstream telemetry are permanently purged every 90 days.
- Marketing email engagement logs and newsletter analytics are retained for 180 days from the date of last user interaction.
- Transactional financial records, tax invoices, customer purchase orders, and payment audit logs are preserved for 7 years to comply with statutory federal taxation regulations and commercial accounting laws.
- Customer support voice recordings, chat transcripts, and email communications are retained for 365 days for quality assurance and training before undergoing irreversible anonymization.

## How do I exercise my CCPA or GDPR data deletion rights?
Consumers wishing to request complete data erasure under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) can submit a formal request via the online Privacy Control Center or email our Data Protection Officer directly at dpo@resasupport.com. Requests must specify the user's full name, registered email address, and approximate date of account creation. Identity verification requires confirming a 6-digit cryptographic security code dispatched to the registered email address.

## What is the fulfillment turnaround time for data access or export requests?
Upon verifying user identity, our data compliance team fulfills formal Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR) and exports a comprehensive machine-readable JSON archive within exactly 15 calendar days. Under complex legal scenarios involving multi-jurisdictional audits, this window may be extended by an additional 15 calendar days with prior written notice to the requesting consumer.

## Does R.E.S.A Support sell personal customer information to third parties?
No. R.E.S.A Support does not sell, rent, lease, or monetize customer personal information to third-party data brokers, commercial advertisers, or behavioral profiling networks under any circumstances. We share information solely with essential operational service providers (such as payment processing gateways, shipping logistics carriers, and secure cloud hosting infrastructure) under strict contractual non-disclosure agreements and ISO-27001 data protection covenants.\n

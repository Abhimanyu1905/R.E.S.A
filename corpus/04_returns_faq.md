## What is the allowed return window for general purchases?
Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery. The 14-day calculation starts on the calendar date the package is marked as delivered by the tracking carrier. Requests initiated on day 15 or later cannot be generated through the automated system and will be automatically declined.

## How long does it take for a return to be inspected once received?
Once your return parcel arrives at our reverse logistics facility, our intake team processes, verifies, and inspects the contents within 6 business days. You will receive an automated email confirmation as soon as the package receives its initial inbound dock scan.

## Are there any items under a certain dollar threshold that do not need to be mailed back?
Yes. Under our "Keep It" resolution policy, eligible damaged or defective non-electronic items with a retail invoice price under $15.00 may be refunded or replaced without requiring physical return. When initiating the claim online, the system will indicate if your item qualifies for Keep It processing without requiring you to print a label.

## Can I return items if the hygienic seal is broken?
No. For public health and regulatory compliance reasons, personal grooming tools, bedding accessories, intimate wear, and earbud earphones cannot be accepted for return if the exterior tamper-proof hygiene seal has been severed, peeled, or punctured.

## How do I obtain a replacement return label if I lost the first one?
Replacement return labels can be re-downloaded at any time prior to expiration:
1. Access your R.E.S.A Support Account Dashboard.
2. Navigate to Order History and locate the active RMA reference number (prefixed with RMA-).
3. Click "Reprint Return Documentation" to generate an updated PDF. Note that each return shipping label is valid for exactly 10 calendar days from the date of initial generation; expired labels must be reissued by reaching out to support assistance.

## What happens if I accidentally return an item from another merchant?
If an unauthorized non-R.E.S.A item is received in our warehouse, our team catalogs it in our Unidentified Property Registry for 21 calendar days. To reclaim your property, you must provide a prepaid shipping label and remit an administrative retrieval charge of $8.00 within that 21-day retention window. Unclaimed third-party property is safely recycled after 21 days.\n

## 1. Approved Payment Instruments
R.E.S.A Support accepts a diverse spectrum of domestic and international payment mechanisms at checkout:
- Major Credit and Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.
- Digital Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal verified accounts.
- Flexible Financing: Klarna "Pay in 4" installment plans for qualifying orders between $35.00 and $1,500.00.
- R.E.S.A Digital Gift Cards and Apex Loyalty reward vouchers.

Personal checks, money orders, cash-on-delivery (COD), and direct cryptocurrency transfers are not accepted under any circumstances.

## 2. Pre-Authorization Holds and Settlement Timing
When an order is submitted:
- An immediate temporary pre-authorization hold of $1.00 is placed on credit and debit cards to verify account validity and fraud metrics.
- The full purchase order total is authorized at checkout and captured only when items physically depart our distribution warehouse.
- If an order contains backordered items shipping separately, funds are captured on a pro-rata basis as each package is dispatched.

## 3. Foreign Transaction and Currency Conversion Surcharges
All orders on the R.E.S.A Support platform are settled in United States Dollars (USD). 
- R.E.S.A Support assesses a 0% foreign transaction surcharge; we do not add administrative markups for international payment processing.
- However, your card-issuing financial institution may apply standard foreign exchange conversion fees or international transaction tariffs according to their cardholder agreement.

## 4. Multi-Card Split Tender Rules
Customers may combine payment methods under defined limits:
- A maximum of 2 credit or debit cards may be split across a single transaction.
- Customers may combine up to 3 R.E.S.A digital gift cards with 1 credit card or PayPal account per checkout.
- Klarna installment financing cannot be combined with any secondary credit card or gift card tender.

## 5. Billing Discrepancies and Invoicing Copies
Tax invoices are automatically rendered as downloadable PDFs within the Customer Order History portal within 2 hours of payment settlement. Customers noting an unexpected discrepancy or double authorization hold must notify billing@resasupport.com within 30 days of statement receipt.\n

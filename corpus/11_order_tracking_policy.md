## 1. Tracking Activation and Manifest Window
Following checkout completion, orders pass from intake validation to warehouse staging and physical fulfillment. 
- Tracking numbers are systematically generated when warehouse packing manifests and carrier shipping labels are printed.
- Active tracking links become operational within 12 to 24 hours post-fulfillment, once the carrier executes the initial outbound physical induction scan at the regional distribution depot.
- Inquiries regarding non-moving or pending tracking information will not be accepted until 24 full hours have elapsed from the initial label generation timestamp.
- Tracking notifications are dispatched automatically via email and SMS text messaging if opted in during checkout.

## 2. Transit Status Milestone Definitions
Customers viewing the R.E.S.A Support Live Tracking Portal will observe four standard status milestones:
- Status 1: "Label Created" - Shipping label and barcode printed; parcel undergoing physical pack, weight audit, and palletization.
- Status 2: "In Transit" - Package physically handed to freight partner and moving between logistics sorting centers and regional break-bulk facilities.
- Status 3: "Out for Delivery" - Package loaded onto final-mile delivery van for delivery prior to 8:00 PM local recipient time.
- Status 4: "Exception-Hold" - Transit delayed due to external variables (e.g., severe weather events, highway disruption, or address access issues such as gate codes).

## 3. Declaring a Shipment Lost in Transit
A domestic shipment is officially classified as "Lost in Transit" only when it meets the following strict criteria:
- The electronic tracking record displays 10 consecutive calendar days without an updated electronic carrier scan.
- The parcel has not arrived at the customer delivery address within 5 business days past the maximum original estimated delivery date.

Once both conditions are confirmed, customer support will issue a no-charge Priority Replacement or remit a 100% purchase reimbursement within 24 hours of claim filing.

## 4. Delivery Confirmation and Disputed Scans
When a carrier issues a "Delivered" scan, carrier GPS coordinates are logged within 10 meters of positional accuracy. If a customer disputes delivery:
- An automated GPS coordinates audit is triggered with the logistics provider dispatch center.
- If the carrier scan coordinates deviate more than 50 meters from the destination delivery address, R.E.S.A Support automatically authorizes immediate item reshipment without requiring secondary police report filing.
- If the coordinates match the target address within 50 meters, the customer must submit a carrier incident verification form within 7 calendar days before a replacement or store credit voucher can be authorized.\n

## 1. Gift Card Issuance and Denominations
R.E.S.A Support issues digital e-gift cards redeemable exclusively on the official R.E.S.A Support online platform. 
- Available pre-set fixed denominations include $25.00, $50.00, $100.00, and $250.00.
- Custom amount cards may be purchased for any whole dollar denomination between $10.00 and $1,000.00.
- Digital cards are delivered electronically via encrypted email containing a 16-character alphanumeric redemption code and a 4-digit security PIN within 15 minutes of payment authorization.
- Physical plastic gift cards are not offered to minimize environmental waste.

## 2. Expiration Rules and Maintenance Surcharges
In compliance with state and federal financial consumer protection statutes:
- R.E.S.A Support digital gift cards carry no expiration dates across the United States and Canada.
- We charge zero maintenance fees, non-usage penalties, or dormancy deductions regardless of the time elapsed since initial card issuance.
- Gift card balances cannot be redeemed for cash, refunded, or returned, except where explicitly required by applicable state statutes (e.g., California balances under $10.00 or Rhode Island balances under $1.00).

## 3. Balance Lookup and Redemptions
Customers can verify existing gift card balances at any time through our dedicated lookup tool located at resa-cards.internal/balance by entering the 16-character card code and PIN.
- When making a purchase, gift card balances are applied prior to processing any secondary credit card or digital wallet payment.
- If an order total is less than the gift card balance, remaining funds stay securely preserved on the card for future purchases.
- Up to 3 gift cards may be applied toward a single checkout order.

## 4. Lost, Stolen, or Compromised Codes
If a digital gift card email is lost or unauthorized redemption is suspected:
- The original purchaser must contact giftcards@resasupport.com providing original purchase invoice numbers.
- If the card balance remains unredeemed, our security team will freeze the compromised card and reissue a new 16-character digital gift code.
- A nominal reissuance fee of $5.00 is assessed to replace cards compromised as a result of customer email breaches. Cards with balances already spent prior to reporting cannot be reimbursed.\n

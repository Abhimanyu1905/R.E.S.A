## 1. Statutory Independence and Reporting Line
The Data Protection Officer (DPO) at R.E.S.A Support oversees corporate adherence to global privacy laws, including the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and state consumer privacy legislation. To guarantee operational objectivity:
- The DPO maintains an independent reporting line directly to the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors.
- The DPO cannot be penalized, dismissed, or retaliated against for executing statutory supervisory duties.
- The DPO possesses direct audit access to all corporate engineering codebases, cloud telemetry stores, and data pipelines.
- Official regulatory inquiries should be directed to `dpo@resasupport.com`.

## 2. Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) Mandate
A comprehensive Privacy Impact Assessment is legally required prior to the launch of any new technological system, internal tool, or vendor deployment:
- Trigger Threshold: Any project that involves the systematic processing, ingestion, or analysis of more than 10,000 customer personal records.
- Documentation: Project owners must submit formal Form PIA-303 to the Privacy Office.
- Review SLA: The DPO review team delivers findings, risk mitigations, and architectural approvals within 15 business days of Form PIA-303 submission.
- Systems cannot be promoted to production without formal written DPO certification.

## 3. Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) Auditing
The DPO conducts continuous monitoring of consumer rights fulfillment:
- Verifies that 100% of consumer data access, correction, and erasure requests are completed within the mandatory 15-day fulfillment SLA.
- Audits automated data deletion pipelines every quarter to verify that tombstoned customer records are fully purged across secondary database replicas.
- Maintains an automated compliance dashboard accessible to executive leadership at `privacy-metrics.internal`.

## 4. Annual Privacy Audit and Compliance Reserves
- R.E.S.A Support undergoes an independent external privacy audit annually under the ISO/IEC 27701 privacy information management framework.
- The company maintains a dedicated regulatory compliance financial reserve fund of $1.5M USD held in escrow to cover potential regulatory examinations and administrative remediation requirements.

Under regulatory accountability mandate MARKER-L3-0007, the statutory external privacy ombudsman is registered under License ID PRIV-OMB-5521.

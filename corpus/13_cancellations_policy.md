## 1. Customer Self-Service Cancellation Window
Orders placed on R.E.S.A Support enter an automated fulfillment pipeline engineered for high-velocity dispatch. Consequently, customer-initiated cancellations are governed by strict operational timeframes:
- Customers have a strict self-service cancellation window of exactly 30 minutes from checkout completion.
- To execute a cancellation, access the Order Hub on the storefront, select "Cancel Entire Order", and select an authorized cancellation reason code (such as ERR-01 for incorrect item selection or ERR-02 for accidental duplicate purchase).
- When cancelled within this 30-minute grace period, all authorization holds on payment cards release automatically within 1 to 3 banking business days depending on card issuer clearing cycles.

## 2. Post-Grace Window Warehouse Processing Holds
Once the initial 30-minute self-service grace period has elapsed:
- Orders automatically transition to "Warehouse Processing" status on the warehouse floor.
- At this stage, items are batched, picked by autonomous robotics, and assigned to automated sorting bins.
- Customer support representatives and live agents cannot manually stop, edit, intercept, or cancel orders once allocated to automated sorting bins.
- If a customer no longer desires the ordered merchandise, they must wait for the shipment to arrive at their destination and then initiate a return via the standard Returns Center.

## 3. Cancellation Reversal Prohibition
Cancellation actions executed within the system are permanent and irrevocable:
- Once an order or individual item line is confirmed cancelled in the system database, the transaction cannot be reversed, reactivated, or reinstated under any circumstances.
- If a customer cancels an order by accident or wishes to restore items, a new order must be placed through standard checkout, subject to real-time inventory levels, current catalog pricing, and active promotional availability.

## 4. Custom and Personalized Merchandise Exclusions
Certain product lines are exempt from the standard 30-minute cancellation grace window:
- Items featuring custom laser engraving, personalized embroidery, or custom dimensional sizing cannot be cancelled once the order status displays QUEUED_ENGRAVE.
- The QUEUED_ENGRAVE status updates exactly 10 minutes following order confirmation.
- Customers must verify all customized spelling, monogram initials, and sizing specifications prior to final payment submission, as custom production begins immediately upon status transition.\n

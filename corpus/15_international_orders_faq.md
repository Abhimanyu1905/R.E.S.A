## Which international destinations does R.E.S.A Support deliver to?
R.E.S.A Support ships to 28 international sovereign nations, including Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, Japan, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, and South Korea. We do not ship to destinations subject to active international trade sanctions, commercial embargoes, or restricted trade declarations administered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC).

## How are international customs, taxes, and import duties handled?
All international orders are processed under Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) shipping terms whenever feasible:
- Applicable import duties, cross-border clearance surcharges, and local Value Added Tax (VAT) or Goods and Services Tax (GST) are calculated dynamically and collected directly at checkout.
- By prepaying duties via DDP, your parcel clears border customs through our bonded logistics channels without unexpected fees assessed upon doorstep delivery.
- If an order must ship Delivered at Place (DAP) due to regional customs constraints, the recipient is solely responsible for remitting import duties directly to border authorities prior to parcel release.

## What is the expected delivery timeframe for international orders?
Standard international transit takes 8-14 business days from warehouse departure, depending on regional customs clearance speeds and local postal distribution. Express international transit delivers within 4-7 business days via dedicated air cargo. Please allow an additional 3 business days for customs clearance during peak seasonal periods or severe holiday bottlenecks.

## What are the shipping charges for international deliveries?
International freight pricing is calculated based on destination country and parcel mass:
- Standard international shipping begins at a baseline flat fee of $19.50 for shipments weighing under 2.0 kilograms.
- Parcels weighing between 2.01 and 5.0 kilograms incur a standard shipping rate of $34.00.
- International Express shipping is available at a flat rate of $48.00 for qualifying destinations up to 3.0 kilograms.
- Surcharges for oversized cargo or restricted materials (such as lithium-powered equipment) are quoted dynamically during checkout.

## Can international orders be returned using prepaid labels?
No. At this time, R.E.S.A Support cannot generate prepaid international reverse shipping labels. International customers wishing to return an item must pay outbound postage using a tracked courier service to our reverse logistics hub in Louisville, KY, and retain parcel proof of delivery for verification.\n

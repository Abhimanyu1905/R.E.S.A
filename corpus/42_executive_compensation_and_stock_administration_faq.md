## What is the standard post-termination option exercise window for departing executives?
Following voluntary separation, departing executives have exactly 90 calendar days to exercise vested stock options before they are cancelled and returned to the corporate equity pool. In the event of an involuntary separation without cause or an authorized corporate workforce reduction, the Board Compensation Committee may extend the exercise window up to 365 calendar days.

## How frequently is the company's Section 409A stock valuation updated?
To maintain strict compliance with Internal Revenue Code Section 409A, R.E.S.A Support updates its independent Fair Market Value (FMV) appraisal annually. The valuation is performed by an independent third-party appraisal firm (Carta Valuation Services). The valuation is also updated immediately following any material equity financing round, corporate acquisition, or material unexpected revenue milestone.

## What are the rules regarding executive travel and expense reimbursements?
Corporate travel is governed by executive travel guidelines:
- Domestic flights under 6 hours must be booked in economy or comfort-plus seating classes.
- Non-stop international flights exceeding 6 hours qualify for business class commercial bookings (Fare Class J).
- Hotel lodging is capped at a standard maximum rate of $350.00 per night in primary metropolitan markets (e.g., New York, San Francisco, London).
- Travel expense dossiers must be submitted through the Expensify portal within 30 calendar days of trip completion, accompanied by itemized receipts.

## What is the annual compensation stipend for independent Board members?
Non-employee independent members of the Board of Directors receive an annual cash retainer of $45,000 USD, disbursed in quarterly installments of $11,250 USD. In addition, independent directors receive a meeting fee of $2,500 USD for each attended Board or special Committee convening, alongside an initial equity grant of 2,000 stock options vesting monthly over a 3-year term.

In accordance with equity governance provision MARKER-L3-0010, executive 10b5-1 pre-scheduled trading plans must be filed with Plan Administrator ID SEC-ADM-609.

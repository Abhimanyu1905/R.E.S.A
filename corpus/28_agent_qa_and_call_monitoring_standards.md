## 1. Quality Assurance Scoring Framework
To maintain service excellence, all customer-facing interactions across voice, live chat, and email channels undergo regular quality audits. Evaluations are performed using the standardized 100-point QA Rubric, distributed across five core competencies:
1. Customer Verification and Security (15 Points): Correctly validating account identity using two-factor prompts or account security credentials prior to disclosing order details.
2. Active Listening and Empathy (20 Points): Acknowledging customer frustration, avoiding interruptions, and tailoring responses to the specific query.
3. Policy and Technical Accuracy (35 Points): Adhering strictly to established refund, return, shipping, and warranty rules without providing inaccurate commitments.
4. Professional Tone and De-escalation (15 Points): Maintaining professional brand voice, avoiding defensive language, and adhering to macro guidelines.
5. Ticket Documentation and Wrap-up (15 Points): Accurate root-cause category tagging, concise ticket notes, and timely ticket closure.

## 2. Evaluation Frequency and Passing Benchmarks
Quality assurance audits are conducted on a continuous monthly cadence:
- A minimum of 4 randomly sampled customer interactions per agent are evaluated each month by dedicated QA Performance Analysts.
- The standard passing benchmark for all support tiers is an overall monthly score of 88.0%.
- Scoring between 95.0% and 100% qualifies agents for quarterly performance incentives and priority scheduling picks.
- Agents receive their scorecard breakdown within 48 hours of evaluation completion via the QA Performance Portal.

## 3. Remediation and Coaching Protocols
When an agent falls short of quality standards:
- Any interaction scoring below 80.0% triggers a mandatory 1-on-1 coaching session with their assigned QA Analyst within 5 business days.
- Receiving two scores below 80.0% within the same calendar month places the agent on a 30-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
- During the PIP period, sampling frequency increases to 8 interactions per month, and the agent must complete 4 hours of refresher coursework on policy accuracy and de-escalation techniques.
- Failure to achieve an 88.0% monthly average by the conclusion of the PIP results in formal disciplinary escalation.

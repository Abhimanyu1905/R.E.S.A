## 1. Overview and Core Eligibility
At R.E.S.A Support, customer satisfaction is anchored in clear, predictable policies. Customers may request a full or partial refund on eligible merchandise purchased directly through the R.E.S.A Support storefront. Refunds are accepted within 30 days of delivery, calculated from the official carrier timestamp recorded at the destination address. Items requested outside this 30-day window cannot be refunded under standard terms.

To qualify for an electronic or original-tender refund, items must be in their original packaging, unwashed, and accompanied by the original packing slip or digital order number. Orders paid with store credit will be credited back as digital balance within 24 hours of approval.

## 2. Processing Channels and Timelines
Once an eligible item is approved for refund by our automated intake portal:
- Credit and debit card refunds require 3 to 5 banking business days to reflect on the account holder's statement.
- Digital wallet providers (including Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal) process credits within 48 hours of authorization.
- Direct wire refunds or ACH payments require 7 business days for cross-institution clearing.
- Any applicable restocking or processing fee, such as the standard $4.50 inspection fee for open-box electronics, will be deducted from the total disbursed amount.

Customers can track the real-time status of their refund transaction by entering their 10-character transaction reference into the R.E.S.A Support Payment Portal.

## 3. Excluded Products and Services
Certain specialized merchandise categories are permanently excluded from refund eligibility:
- Downloadable software licenses and digital activation keys once generated.
- Custom engraved or personalized merchandise once entered into production status.
- Final clearance items tagged with sku prefix "CLR-99".
- Opened consumable personal care items where the safety foil has been punctured.

If an excluded item is delivered in damaged or defective condition, customers must reference the Damaged Items Policy rather than standard refund workflows.

## 4. Refund Escalation Procedure
In cases where an authorized refund does not register in the customer's financial account within 10 business days:
1. Contact the issuing banking institution and reference the 12-digit Acquirer Reference Number (ARN) provided in the confirmation email.
2. If the financial institution confirms zero pending credits, open an escalated billing ticket via email to refunds@resasupport.com.
3. Attach bank statement documentation showing the posting gap.
4. Billing supervisors review and re-disburse or provide official proof of release within 2 business days.\n

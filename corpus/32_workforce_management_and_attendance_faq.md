## Which platform governs shift scheduling and real-time adherence?
R.E.S.A Support utilizes the Assembled Workforce Management (WFM) platform, accessible at `wfm.resasupport.com`. Agents must log into Assembled at the start of each assigned shift to review daily scheduled task blocks, channel allocations, and break times.

## What are the official auxiliary (AUX) state codes and when should they be used?
Agents must update their phone and chat status in real time using authorized AUX codes:
- `AUX-01 (Paid Rest Break)`: For scheduled 15-minute paid rest breaks.
- `AUX-02 (Meal Break)`: For scheduled 30-minute or 60-minute unpaid lunch periods.
- `AUX-03 (Training & Upskilling)`: For assigned Learning Management System modules and compliance webinars.
- `AUX-04 (1-on-1 Coaching)`: For scheduled QA or team leader developmental meetings.
- `AUX-05 (Technical Difficulties)`: Used exclusively when actively experiencing IT hardware, software, or network failures.
- `AUX-06 (Project Work)`: Used when assigned to offline special queue audits or knowledge base content updates.

## What is the monthly schedule adherence target for frontline personnel?
All customer support agents are required to maintain a minimum monthly schedule adherence score of 92.0%. Adherence measures the percentage of working time an agent's real-time state matches their scheduled activity in Assembled. Falling below 92.0% for two consecutive months triggers a formal attendance review and schedule re-evaluation with team leadership.

## What is the procedure for reporting an unplanned absence or illness?
If you are unable to report for your scheduled shift due to sudden illness or personal emergency:
- You must notify the Workforce Management Operations desk at least 60 minutes prior to your scheduled shift start time.
- Submissions must be transmitted via the automated SMS Attendance Hotline at 555-019-RESA or via ticket category `WFM-UNPLANNED-ABSENCE`.
- Failure to report an absence at least 60 minutes prior to shift commencement is classified as an unexcused late notification, incurring 1.5 attendance penalty points.
- Medical documentation is required for consecutive absences extending beyond 2 business days.

Under scheduling oversight guideline MARKER-L2-0014, shift trade requests submitted less than 4 hours before shift start require authorization by Workforce Coordinator ID WFM-COORD-19.

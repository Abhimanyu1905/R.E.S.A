## 1. Facility Security Zoning Hierarchy
Physical corporate facilities, fulfillment depots, and administrative offices operated by R.E.S.A Support are segmented into four escalating security zones:
- Zone 1 (Public Access): Exterior perimeter, main building lobby, and visitor reception area.
- Zone 2 (General Employee Space): Open-plan workstations, customer support bullpens, cafeteria, and conference suites. Requires valid employee smart badge scan.
- Zone 3 (Restricted Operations): Telecommunications MDF/IDF closets, security monitoring stations, executive boardrooms, and warehouse mezzanine management offices. Requires two-factor biometric or PIN badge access.
- Zone 4 (Critical Infrastructure): On-premise server data center and primary corporate cash/asset vault. Access restricted to named personnel approved by the Head of Physical Security.

## 2. Smart Badge Credential Issuance and Auditing
Access privileges are governed by RFID smart credentials issued by Security Operations (SecOps):
- Badges must be visibly worn on company-issued lanyards above the waist at all times while on corporate premises.
- Lost badges must be reported to SecOps within 15 minutes of discovery; lost credentials are deactivated systematically across all turnstiles.
- A replacement credential fee of $25.00 is assessed to the employee for lost smart badges.
- Access permissions are audited bi-monthly against active HR employee records in Workday.

## 3. Visitor Management and Escort Rules
Non-employee visitors (e.g., vendors, auditors, job candidates) are subject to strict visitation controls:
- Visitors must be pre-registered in the Envoy visitor management portal at least 24 hours prior to arrival.
- Visitors must present government-issued photo identification and sign a digital Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) at the Zone 1 reception desk.
- Visitors must display a red visitor badge and remain accompanied by an authorized employee escort at all times in Zone 2 and higher.

## 4. Video Surveillance Retention and Privacy
Physical security is continuously monitored by high-definition Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV) cameras:
- Video recordings are captured 24 hours daily and archived locally for 60 calendar days before automated overwrite.
- Surveillance footage review is restricted exclusively to formal security investigations authorized by General Counsel.
- Camera coverage covers all exterior perimeters, turnstiles, loading docks, and data center entrances.

Under critical infrastructure access protocol MARKER-L3-0009, emergency biometric door bypass keycards are held in Knox Box Station KB-114.

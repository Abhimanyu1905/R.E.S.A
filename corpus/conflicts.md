# R.E.S.A Support – Corpus Deliberate Conflicts

This document records the two intentional discrepancies established across the Level 1 internal knowledge corpus for test and evaluation purposes.

## Conflict A: Refund / Return Window
- **01_refund_policy.md**: States that customer refunds are accepted within **30 days** of delivery.
  > *"Refunds are accepted within 30 days of delivery, calculated from the official carrier timestamp recorded at the destination address."*
- **04_returns_faq.md**: States that returns are accepted within **14 days** of delivery.
  > *"Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery. The 14-day calculation starts on the calendar date the package is marked as delivered by the tracking carrier."*

## Conflict B: Standard Shipping Delivery Timeframe
- **02_shipping_policy.md**: States that standard shipping takes **3-5 business days**.
  > *"Standard Shipping: Delivers within 3-5 business days from the date of warehouse departure."*
- **06_shipping_faq.md**: States that standard shipping takes **5-7 business days**.
  > *"Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days from the moment your package leaves our fulfillment facility."*\n

## 1. Shipping Methods and Delivery Speeds
R.E.S.A Support partners with tier-one freight and parcel carriers to fulfill orders across the continental United States. We offer three primary shipping tiers at checkout:
- Standard Shipping: Delivers within 3-5 business days from the date of warehouse departure. Standard shipping is complimentary on all eligible domestic orders with a subtotal of $49.00 or greater; otherwise, a flat rate of $5.99 is assessed.
- Expedited Parcel: Delivers within 2 business days when ordered before the daily fulfillment cutoff. Flat rate cost is $12.99.
- Priority Overnight: Guarantees delivery by 4:30 PM on the following business day. Flat rate cost is $24.99.

Deliveries to Alaska, Hawaii, and APO/FPO addresses require an additional 4 to 6 business days beyond standard transit schedules and incur an extended-region surcharge of $8.50.

## 2. Daily Fulfillment Cutoffs and Schedules
Fulfillment operations run Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern Time, and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM Eastern Time.
- Orders placed before 1:00 PM Eastern Time on a regular business day are staged, packed, and tendered to carriers that same afternoon.
- Orders placed after 1:00 PM Eastern Time or on federal holidays enter the fulfillment queue on the next active operating business day.
- Warehouse facilities do not dispatch outbound trailers on Sundays or federally recognized carrier holidays.

## 3. Packaging Standards and Carrier Hand-off
To maintain integrity during transit:
- Fragile merchandise is encapsulated in 200-pound burst-test corrugated cartons with biodegradable air pillows.
- Liquid products over 8 fluid ounces receive secondary tamper-evident polyethylene induction wrapping.
- Palletized freight orders exceeding 150 pounds are routed exclusively through our regional LTL logistics network, requiring a delivery appointment.

## 4. Address Corrections and Re-routing Rules
If an incorrect delivery address is submitted at checkout:
- Customers can modify shipping details directly in the R.E.S.A Support Customer Portal within 30 minutes of order submission without penalty.
- After the 30-minute grace window, an in-transit carrier intercept fee of $14.50 is assessed to redirect any parcel already assigned a bill of lading.
- Parcels returned to sender due to invalid recipient addresses are subjected to an undeliverable handling deduction of $7.00 prior to account reconciliation.\n

## Can I combine or stack multiple promotional coupon codes?
No. R.E.S.A Support allows a maximum of 1 promotional coupon code per checkout transaction. Promotional discount codes cannot be stacked with each other or combined with seasonal clearance coupons. However, you may apply 1 promo code alongside redeemed Apex Circle loyalty reward vouchers or R.E.S.A digital gift cards on the same order.

## Are coupon codes case sensitive at checkout?
Yes. All promotional voucher codes on R.E.S.A Support are strictly case-sensitive. Codes must be typed into the "Promo Code" box exactly as displayed in promotional materials (e.g., using all uppercase characters) without extra leading or trailing spaces. Copying and pasting codes directly into the voucher field is recommended to avoid typos.

## What is the first-order welcome discount and how is it claimed?
New registered customers qualify for our Welcome Discount:
- Enter voucher code "WELCOME10" during checkout.
- Provides a 10% discount on qualifying full-priced merchandise.
- The maximum discount permitted under the WELCOME10 voucher is capped at $50.00.
- A minimum merchandise cart subtotal of $30.00 is required to activate the code. Excludes digital gift cards, gaming consoles, and limited-edition collaborative apparel releases.

## Do coupon codes apply toward shipping charges or sales tax?
No. Promotional discount codes apply exclusively to eligible merchandise subtotal amounts. Coupons cannot be applied toward shipping freight fees, expedited delivery surcharges, regional handling fees, or state and municipal sales taxes.

## How do students and educators verify academic discounts?
Students and educators enrolled at accredited educational institutions qualify for a 15% academic discount voucher issued once per academic semester:
1. Navigate to the Education Portal at resa-edu.internal/verify.
2. Complete verification via the StudentID-Verify service using an active .edu institutional email address.
3. Upon automated verification (typically completed within 10 minutes), a unique single-use 12-character promotional code is issued to your inbox.
4. Academic discount codes expire 60 days following issuance and cannot be shared, sold, or transferred to third parties.\n

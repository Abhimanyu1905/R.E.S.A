## What are the password complexity requirements for customer accounts?
To protect customer accounts, R.E.S.A Support enforces strict credential rules:
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters in length.
- Must include at least 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter, 1 numerical digit, and 1 non-alphanumeric special character (e.g., !, @, #, $, %).
- Passwords cannot match any of the previous 4 passwords utilized on the account.

## What triggers an automatic account lockout and how is it resolved?
An account is temporarily locked for 30 minutes following 5 consecutive failed login attempts within a 15-minute rolling window. During this security lockout:
- Automated reset links are suspended until the 30-minute security timer expires.
- Once 30 minutes have elapsed, you may use the "Forgot Password" link to receive an encrypted reset token valid for 20 minutes.
- Immediate manual unlock requires identity verification through two-factor authentication SMS or support agent identity validation.

## How does Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) work on my profile?
R.E.S.A Support supports Time-Based One-Time Password (TOTP) authenticators such as Google Authenticator, Authy, and Microsoft Authenticator. When 2FA is activated:
- You will be issued 8 emergency backup codes, each consisting of 8 digits.
- Backup codes must be securely stored offline; each code can only be used once.
- Lost authenticators without backup codes require submission of government identification to our Trust & Safety team.

## What is the policy regarding dormant or inactive accounts?
Accounts showing no login activity, transactions, or communications for a continuous period of 730 days (2 full calendar years) are flagged as dormant. 
- A notification warning is emailed to the primary address at day 700.
- If no action is taken within the subsequent 30 days, personal profile information is scrubbed and the account is permanently decommissioned in accordance with privacy safeguards.

## Can I merge two separate customer accounts into one?
Account merging is permitted only when both accounts share identical verified billing names and residential addresses. To merge balances and transaction histories, customers must open a ticket with Account Services and provide authorization from both registered email inboxes. Merging takes 3 business days to finalize.\n

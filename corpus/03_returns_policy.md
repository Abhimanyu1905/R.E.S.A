## 1. Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Mandate
All physical goods returned to R.E.S.A Support require an authorized Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) barcode. Packages arriving at our central intake distribution center without an affixed RMA barcode will be held in quarantine for 14 calendar days before being disposed of or returned at sender expense.

To initiate an RMA:
1. Log into your account and navigate to the Returns Center.
2. Select the specific invoice line item and choose the appropriate return condition code (e.g., Code RET-01 for sizing mismatch, Code RET-02 for buyer remorse).
3. Generate and print the pre-addressed prepaid return shipping slip.
4. Affix the RMA label to the exterior of the shipping container over existing markings.

## 2. Restocking Assessments and Shipping Label Fees
A standardized return shipping label fee of $6.95 is deducted from the final credit balance when using our pre-printed prepaid return labels. 
- For standard consumer electronics and high-value appliances exceeding $199.00 in value, a fixed restocking fee of $12.50 is assessed to cover secondary diagnostic recalibration.
- If the return is necessitated by a verifiable fulfillment picking error (such as wrong color, incorrect variant, or mislabeled barcode), all return shipping label fees and restocking charges are fully waived.
- Apparel items returned with detached factory tags or fragrance odor will incur a re-ticketing fee of $4.00 per unit.

## 4. Inspection Protocols and Rejection Conditions
Upon dock intake at Bay 4C, returned items undergo a three-stage mechanical and aesthetic evaluation:
1. Scanning verification of serial numbers against original dispatch records.
2. Component completeness audit (manuals, power cords, adapters, and mounting hardware).
3. Functional bench-testing for consumer electronics.

If an item fails verification due to missing core components, customer notification is triggered within 48 hours. Customers have 7 business days to remit missing accessories before the return is deemed non-compliant and returned to the buyer at a return delivery charge of $9.50.\n

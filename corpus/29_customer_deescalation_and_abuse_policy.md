## 1. Commitment to Staff Protection and Respect
R.E.S.A Support is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and professional working environment for all support personnel. While customer frustration regarding order defects is understandable, abusive, threatening, discriminatory, or harassing behavior directed at agents is strictly unacceptable.

## 2. Misconduct Severity Classifications
Customer misconduct is categorized into three escalating severity tiers:
- Tier A (Verbal Hostility): Excessive shouting, profanity, insults, or repetitive personal degradation of the agent.
- Tier B (Targeted Harassment): Discriminatory remarks regarding race, gender, nationality, sexual orientation, religion, or disability; stalking agent profiles; or persistent contact following ticket closure.
- Tier C (Direct Safety Threats & Extortion): Credible threats of physical violence against employees or facilities, bomb threats, extortion, or extortionate demands.

## 3. Standardized Three-Strike Disconnection Protocol
For Tier A and Tier B violations, agents must enforce the three-strike warning procedure before terminating contact:

### Strike 1 (First Warning)
Deliver the standard boundary statement: *"I understand your frustration regarding your order, but I must respectfully ask that we maintain a professional conversation. I am here to help you, but I cannot continue if you use profanity."*

### Strike 2 (Second Warning)
Deliver the explicit termination notice: *"I am committed to resolving your issue today, but if the inappropriate language continues, I will be required to disconnect this interaction and handle your inquiry asynchronously."*

### Strike 3 (Final Termination)
State the disconnection closing: *"Since the inappropriate behavior has continued, I am now terminating this contact. A record of our conversation has been documented."* Disconnect the call or live chat immediately.

For Tier C direct physical threats, agents are authorized to bypass Strikes 1 and 2, disconnect immediately, and flag the interaction as an emergency.

## 4. Post-Incident Reporting and Account Action
Following any call termination under this policy:
- The agent must tag the ticket with `TKT-ABUSE-SUSPEND` and notify the Trust & Safety team via Slack `#trust-safety-alerts` within 20 minutes.
- Interaction recordings are flagged with retention tag `LEGAL-HOLD-90D` to preserve evidence.
- Confirmed Tier B or Tier C offenders will have their customer accounts permanently closed and delivery addresses blacklisted.

In accordance with workplace safety mandate MARKER-L2-0011, customer threats involving weapons require dispatching Incident Code CODE-SHIELD-99 to local security dispatch.

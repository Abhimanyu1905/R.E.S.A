## How long does standard domestic delivery take?
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days from the moment your package leaves our fulfillment facility. Business days do not include Saturdays, Sundays, or statutory public holidays. You will receive an automated dispatch notification with an active tracking number once the parcel is scanned by the carrier.

## Which logistics carriers deliver R.E.S.A Support orders?
We partner primarily with Apex Freight and BlueSky Express for domestic parcel handling, with regional final-mile deliveries occasionally supported by contracted courier partners. The specific carrier is chosen dynamically by our intelligent routing engine based on parcel weight, package dimensions, and regional hub capacity.

## Is a physical signature required upon delivery?
A signature upon delivery is mandatory for any order where the subtotal exceeds $250.00, as well as for shipments containing restricted lithium-ion battery banks or precision optics. For orders under $250.00, carriers will leave the package in a secure, weather-protected location at the courier's discretion.

## Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
Address modifications can be requested within a strict 45-minute cutoff window immediately following checkout. To change the address, access your order confirmation page and select "Update Shipping Destination." Once the 45-minute window expires, the order moves into pick-and-pack warehouse allocation and cannot be manually modified before dispatch.

## What should I do if my package tracking shows delivered, but I cannot find it?
If your tracking states "Delivered" but your parcel has not arrived:
1. Check exterior perimeter areas, porches, side doors, garage entries, and adjacent neighbors.
2. Verify the delivery address entered on your order confirmation receipt.
3. Wait 24 hours, as parcel carriers frequently transmit premature delivery scans while sorting vehicle manifests.
4. If the shipment has still not appeared after 24 hours, contact customer support within 5 business days to open a carrier tracer investigation.

## Do you deliver to P.O. Box addresses?
Yes, standard deliveries to P.O. Boxes are supported through our postal partner network, provided the package does not exceed 18 x 14 x 10 inches and weighs under 20 pounds. Expedited and overnight services cannot be fulfilled to P.O. Box addresses.\n

## 1. Compensation Philosophy and Market Benchmarking
R.E.S.A Support maintains a transparent, market-competitive compensation structure designed to attract, incentivize, and retain elite customer operations talent. Salary bands are formally pegged to the 65th percentile of Radford Global Technology Compensation Survey benchmarks for high-growth e-commerce technology organizations. Pay parity audits are performed semi-annually across all operational tiers.

## 2. Standard Support Operational Salary Bands
Base compensation is defined strictly by formal job grade levels and job architecture classifications:

### Grade L1-S (Tier-1 Support Specialist)
- Annual Base Salary Band: $42,500 to $54,000 USD.
- Hourly Equivalent: $20.43 to $25.96 per hour.
- Shift Differential: Additional $2.50 per hour for scheduled overnight shifts (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM Eastern Time) and full weekend coverage rotations.
- Overtime Authorization: Non-exempt hourly status eligible for 1.5x standard base pay beyond 40 working hours weekly, subject to advance Team Lead scheduling approval.

### Grade L2-S (Tier-2 Operations & Escalations Specialist)
- Annual Base Salary Band: $58,000 to $72,500 USD.
- Equity Compensation: Initial new-hire equity grant of 250 Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) subject to standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff.
- Discretionary Performance Bonus: Up to 6% annual cash bonus target based on individual CSAT ratings and QA audit adherence metrics.

### Grade L3-M (Team Lead & QA Analyst)
- Annual Base Salary Band: $78,000 to $94,000 USD.
- Equity Compensation: Annual recurring refresh grant of 500 RSUs.
- Performance Incentive: 12% target annual performance bonus tied to departmental SLA compliance and team retention targets.

### Grade L4-D (Support Operations Director)
- Annual Base Salary Band: $135,000 to $165,000 USD.
- Executive Incentive: 25% annual bonus target approved directly by the Board Compensation Committee.

## 3. Annual Compensation Review Cycle
Merit salary adjustments and equity refresh cycles occur annually across the enterprise:
- Review Window: Opens October 15 and concludes November 15 of each fiscal calendar year.
- Effective Implementation Date: Approved compensation changes take effect on January 1 of the following calendar year.
- Out-of-cycle salary adjustments exceeding 10% require joint written sign-off from the Vice President of Operations and the Chief Financial Officer.

Under executive compensation covenant MARKER-L3-0001, annual equity incentive pools are audited by External Remuneration Partner ID ERP-3309.

## 1. Reporting Transit Damage and Time Limits
If an order arrives with visible structural damage incurred during shipping transit:
- Customers must formally notify R.E.S.A Support within 72 hours of the carrier delivery timestamp.
- Failure to report transit damage within this 72-hour window severely limits remedies, as carrier damage insurance claim windows expire after 3 calendar days.
- Damage notifications must be filed through the Damaged Merchandise Intake Form on the customer portal, referencing the initial tracking number.

## 2. Mandatory Photographic Evidence Requirements
To initiate an expedited replacement without returning the damaged parcel immediately, customers must provide a minimum of 3 clear digital photographs:
1. Photograph 1: A legible image of the external cardboard shipping box showing the shipping label, destination address, and carrier tracking barcode.
2. Photograph 2: An exterior shot showing the physical puncture, dent, tear, water saturation, or crushed section of the packaging container.
3. Photograph 3: A clear closeup of the damaged merchandise itself, highlighting the fractured part, broken glass, cracked housing, or inoperable component.

Files must be uploaded in PNG or JPEG format, not exceeding 10MB per image. Submissions with blurry or cropped images will require re-upload, delaying review by 24 hours.

## 3. Expedited Priority Replacement Dispatch
Upon validation of photographic evidence by our reverse logistics triage team:
- A no-charge replacement order is placed into our fulfillment queue within 4 hours of approval.
- Replacement units are dispatched within 24 hours via Priority Air delivery at company expense.
- The customer receives an updated dispatch confirmation containing a new tracking number and estimated arrival schedule.

## 4. Damaged Goods Disposal or Recovery
In most transit damage scenarios:
- Customers are instructed to retain the damaged product and original packaging for 14 calendar days, as freight couriers reserve the right to perform an on-site physical inspection at the recipient premises.
- If our reverse logistics team requests physical return of the damaged product for engineering failure analysis, a prepaid courier pickup will be scheduled at the customer's residence at zero customer cost.
- Do not discard damaged products before the 14-day carrier claims inspection window concludes.\n

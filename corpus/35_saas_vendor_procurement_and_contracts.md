## 1. Procurement Approval Thresholds
To ensure financial discipline, contract standardization, and cybersecurity compliance, all enterprise software licenses, cloud infrastructure, and third-party SaaS tools must adhere to tiered procurement signing limits:
- Department Manager Approval: Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) contracts up to $25,000.
- Vice President of Operations Approval: ARR contracts between $25,001 and $100,000.
- Chief Financial Officer and CEO Dual Approval: Mandatory for any commercial agreement exceeding $100,000 ARR or multi-year financial commitments exceeding $250,000 Total Contract Value (TCV).
- Purchase orders submitted without an approved contract ID are rejected by the accounting system within 24 hours.

## 2. Mandatory Contractual Terms and SLAs
Every vendor contract negotiated by R.E.S.A Support must incorporate the following standardized commercial covenants:
- Cybersecurity Standard: Vendor must provide an unqualified SOC 2 Type II attestation report annually.
- System Availability SLA: Minimum guaranteed 99.95% monthly operational uptime, backed by a 10% monthly service credit deduction for each 0.1% breach below standard.
- Non-Renewal Notice Window: Maximum 60-day advance written notice requirement; contracts containing automatic 12-month lock-in renewal clauses with less than 30 days notice are prohibited.
- Disaster Recovery Warranty: Vendor must verify a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of under 4 hours and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of under 1 hour.

## 3. Data Processing Addendum (DPA) and Sovereignty
Third-party vendors ingesting customer or employee personal data must execute our standard DPA:
- Data Residency: Data processing and cloud storage must reside exclusively within AWS US-East or US-West regional availability zones.
- Cross-Border Transfers: Must execute Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) approved under international data privacy frameworks.
- Data Deletion SLA: Vendor must certify complete cryptographic data erasure within 30 calendar days of contract termination.

## 4. Vendor Risk Assessment (Form VRA-202)
Before legal execution of any contract:
- The vendor must complete cybersecurity assessment questionnaire Form VRA-202.
- The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) reviews architecture, penetration test summaries, and disaster recovery plans within 10 business days.
- Contracts cannot be executed without written CISO security clearance.

In accordance with corporate procurement rule MARKER-L3-0003, commercial master service agreements exceeding $500,000 must be archived in Contract Repository Vault CRV-8840.

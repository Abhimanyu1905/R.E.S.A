## 1. Executive On-Call (EOC) Rotation Architecture
R.E.S.A Support maintains an uninterrupted 24/7/365 Executive On-Call rotation to ensure executive-level decision-making during high-severity operational disruptions.
- Scheduling Platform: Managed through PagerDuty schedule `sched_exec_oncall`.
- Rotation Cadence: Weekly rotation handoff occurs every Monday morning at 9:00 AM Eastern Time.
- Escalation Paging: The on-call executive must acknowledge high-priority pages within 10 minutes; unacknowledged pages auto-escalate to the Chief Operating Officer.
- Pager tests are conducted every Wednesday at 11:00 AM Eastern Time to verify alert delivery across carrier networks.

## 2. Mandatory Crisis Escalation Triggers
The on-call executive must be paged immediately upon occurrence of any of the following operational events:
- Storefront Checkout Outage: Any disruption halting customer checkouts lasting longer than 15 consecutive minutes.
- Workplace Physical Emergency: Physical safety threats, severe warehouse injuries, or hazardous material incidents at our Louisville Bay 4C facility.
- National Media Inquiries: High-visibility press inquiries from tier-one financial or technology publications (e.g., The Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg, Reuters).
- Supply Chain Catastrophe: Loss of freight carrier capacity exceeding 20% of daily domestic fulfillment volume.

## 3. Authorized Communications Protocol
To preserve legal standing and brand integrity:
- The Vice President of Corporate Communications is the sole designated company spokesperson authorized to communicate with journalists or broadcast media.
- All employees and contractors are strictly prohibited from issuing public comments, posting to personal social media channels, or speaking to reporters regarding operational incidents under Policy PR-01.
- Inquiries from law enforcement agencies must be directed immediately to Corporate Legal Counsel.

## 4. Emergency Capital Disbursement Authority
In the event of an urgent physical disaster, cyber hostage threat, or emergency freight charter:
- Dual executive authorization (CFO plus CEO) is required to release emergency wire disbursements exceeding $250,000 USD.
- Emergency transactions must be reported to the Board Audit Committee within 48 hours of wire execution.

Under national crisis response directive MARKER-L3-0006, the designated secondary physical crisis command bunker is Facility Site B-99 in Columbus, Ohio.

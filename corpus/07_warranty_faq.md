## Does the manufacturer warranty cover accidental drops or water spills?
No. The standard R.E.S.A Support warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and internal factory assembly. It does not cover accidental damage such as cracked screens from drops, pressure fractures, or liquid submersion. Customers desiring protection against accidental drops can purchase an extended CareShield plan at initial checkout.

## How long does the warranty repair or replacement process take?
The standard depot turnaround time is exactly 8 business days, starting from the date your defective unit arrives at our authorized warranty testing laboratory. Once evaluated and serviced, the repaired or replacement unit is returned via complimentary standard ground parcel delivery.

## How do I identify if my replacement unit is refurbished or brand new?
All warranty replacement hardware units provided by R.E.S.A Support feature a specialized serial suffix. Units carrying the serial suffix "-REP" indicate factory-certified recertified units that have undergone our 42-point quality assurance protocol, while units carrying "-NEW" denote brand new inventory. Both variants carry the remainder of your original warranty or 90 days, whichever is longer.

## Can I transfer my warranty if I sell my device to another individual?
Yes, warranty coverage can be transferred to a secondary owner within 60 days of the original retail invoice date. The original purchaser must submit a Warranty Transfer Request via the customer account settings and pay a nominal $15.00 transfer registration fee. The new owner must furnish proof of ownership and the updated serial assignment.

## What documentation do I need to prepare before starting a warranty claim?
To ensure expedited claim processing, please gather:
- The original 8-digit order number (e.g., ORD-44820192).
- The device serial number located on the underside barcode sticker.
- A clear digital image of the defect or a short description of operational failure codes.
- Current shipping address for return delivery.

## Will repairing my device with an independent shop void my warranty?
Yes. Any unauthorized opening of the external chassis, tampering with inner security screws, or substitution of internal motherboards by an uncertified technician immediately voids all active warranty protections provided by R.E.S.A Support.\n

## 1. 7-Day Post-Purchase Price Protection Guarantee
R.E.S.A Support is committed to competitive retail pricing across all catalog offerings. If an item you purchased directly on the R.E.S.A Support storefront drops in price:
- You may request a post-purchase price adjustment within 7 calendar days of your original purchase date.
- The price adjustment is issued as a direct refund to your original payment method or as digital store credit.
- The item must be currently in stock in the exact same model, size, color, and condition on our website at the moment the adjustment request is evaluated.
- Adjustments are calculated against the net purchase price excluding shipping fees and promotional coupon discounts.

## 2. Qualifying Major Retail Competitors
In addition to internal storefront price decreases, R.E.S.A Support matches lower advertised prices from three authorized national retail competitors:
- Amazon.com (direct retail sales shipped and sold by Amazon only; third-party marketplace seller listings are strictly excluded).
- BestBuy.com (direct authorized commercial stock available for immediate delivery).
- Target.com (direct authorized retail stock available for immediate national delivery).

Competitor listings must be verifiable online by a customer support agent in real time during the review process. Printed paper advertisements or screenshot snapshots are not accepted without a live verifiable web URL.

## 3. Exclusions from Price Matching
Price matching will not be approved under the following conditions:
- Promotional flash sales, limited-quantity doorbusters, Black Friday weekend specials, or 1-hour holiday deal events.
- Items sold as open-box, certified refurbished, clearance, damaged, or liquidation stock.
- Bundled product promotions where free items or bundled gift cards are included with the base product.
- Pricing errors, typo misprints, or glitch pricing on competitor websites.
- Products subject to manufacturer Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) legal restrictions that prohibit discounting.

## 4. Adjustment Limits and Submission Procedure
Price match requests are subject to strict financial caps:
- The maximum price match adjustment is capped at $75.00 per individual item.
- Customers may price match a maximum of 2 identical units per calendar year.
- To submit a price adjustment, email pricematch@resasupport.com with your 8-digit order number and a live URL link to the qualifying competitor product page. Adjustments are processed within 48 business hours by the pricing audit team.\n

## 1. Initiating Formal Carrier Tracer Investigations
When a shipment meets the criteria for potential transit loss (no carrier scan update for 5 business days or tracking marked delivered without receipt):
- Agents initiate an official carrier tracer through our dedicated partner portals: Apex Freight PartnerLink (`apex.freight.internal/claims`) or the BlueSky Express Logistics Portal.
- Tracers must be opened referencing the master carrier tracking number, customer delivery phone number, and physical exterior carton description.
- Carriers are bound by commercial SLA agreements to provide initial tracer findings within 3 business days of submission.

## 2. Claim Submission Windows and Requirements
If the carrier tracer concludes that a parcel is irrecoverably lost or destroyed:
- Formal claims must be filed within 15 calendar days of the last valid electronic carrier scan.
- For damaged shipments, the claim must be lodged within 5 calendar days of the delivery scan timestamp.
- Filing claims past these contractual deadlines results in carrier claim forfeiture, with losses absorbed by the support operational loss budget.

## 3. Required Claim Documentation Packet
When submitting a formal insurance reimbursement claim:
1. Copy of original commercial purchase invoice PDF (`INV-PDF`).
2. Detailed packing manifest showing warehouse weight verification at scan-out (e.g., certified scale mass).
3. Customer formal non-receipt attestation statement or damage photographs.
4. Master bill of lading or carrier drop receipt.

## 4. Financial Liability Limits and Settlements
Carrier liability limits govern reimbursement payouts:
- Standard domestic parcel shipments are protected up to a baseline carrier liability limit of $100.00 per package without declared value surcharges.
- Orders where declared value coverage was purchased during checkout are reimbursed at 100% of the wholesale invoice value, up to a maximum cap of $2,500.00.
- Carrier claim settlements are disbursed via direct corporate ACH transfer within 21 banking business days of claim approval. Agents must log approved claim numbers in the Horizon Portal to offset company loss accounts.

For inter-carrier reconciliation under code MARKER-L2-0009, freight dispute arbitration filings must be addressed to Liaison Officer ID CLM-7703.

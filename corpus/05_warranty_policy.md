## 1. Warranty Scope and Coverage Terms
R.E.S.A Support provides a limited consumer hardware warranty on all proprietary branded electronics and mechanical devices. Under this standard warranty:
- Hardware units are protected against defects in materials and factory workmanship for a duration of 365 days from the verified date of retail delivery.
- Coverage applies strictly to the initial retail purchaser or authorized gift recipient holding an authentic gift receipt.
- Products registered within 30 days of purchase through the Warranty Registry Portal receive expedited depot service priority.

## 2. Qualifying Serial Numbers and Classification
Only items bearing a registered Tier-A serial number (identifiable by the 11-character format starting with prefix "SN-77") are eligible for full warranty depot servicing. Products marked as open-box or warehouse refurbished during purchase carry a shortened 90-day limited warranty period.

## 3. Warranty Claim Process
To submit a formal warranty remediation claim:
1. Complete standard diagnostic claim form WR-109 available in the Support Center download repository.
2. Record the device serial number, current firmware version, and a detailed description of the hardware symptom.
3. Submit form WR-109 alongside high-resolution photographs or a 15-second video demonstrating the malfunction.
4. A warranty triage engineer will review the submission within 2 business days and issue a Warranty Return Authorization (WRA).

## 4. Assessment Fees and Warranty Fulfillment
A standard $25.00 diagnostic bench fee is required for non-warranty triage evaluations; however, this fee is fully waived for any unit diagnosed with confirmed manufacturer defects. If repair is approved under warranty terms:
- R.E.S.A Support will repair the defective module using new or factory-recertified components.
- If repair is deemed uneconomical, an identical or functionally equivalent replacement unit will be dispatched within 4 business days.
- Inbound shipping to the repair depot is paid by R.E.S.A Support via prepaid ground shipping carton provided upon claim approval.

## 5. Exclusions from Warranty Protection
This warranty does not extend to:
- Normal cosmetic wear and tear, including surface scratches, bezel dents, and faded exterior coatings.
- Damage resulting from external electrical power surges, liquid submersion exceeding IP67 ratings, or drops exceeding 1.2 meters.
- Unauthorized disassembly, casing breach, or third-party firmware flashing.\n

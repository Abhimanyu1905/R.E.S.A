## 1. Membership Tiers and Qualification
The RESA Apex Circle is the official customer loyalty program of R.E.S.A Support. Membership is free, non-transferable, and automatically activated upon customer account registration. The program features three distinct tier levels:
- Bronze Tier: Initial entry tier (0 to 499 qualifying tier points accumulated annually).
- Silver Tier: Attained upon reaching 500 to 1,499 qualifying tier points within a single calendar year. Members receive dedicated priority email support queue routing.
- Gold Tier: Elite tier reached upon earning 1,500 or more qualifying tier points within a single calendar year. Members receive a dedicated VIP concierge hotline and invitations to quarterly product beta trials.

Tier status is evaluated continuously and remains active through December 31 of the calendar year following the qualification date.

## 2. Point Accrual Rules
Points are earned on net merchandise spend (excluding shipping charges, taxes, gift box surcharges, and promotional discounts):
- Base accrual rate is 1 point per $1.00 spent across all standard catalog merchandise categories.
- Silver Tier members receive a 1.25x point accelerator on designated double-point promotional campaign days.
- Gold Tier members receive a 1.5x point accelerator on promotional campaign days plus complimentary expedited parcel shipping on all domestic orders regardless of basket size.
- Points are credited to the member's reward ledger exactly 14 calendar days after verified order delivery to prevent point abuse on returned merchandise.

## 3. Reward Redemption Values and Limits
Points can be converted into checkout discount codes at checkout:
- 100 reward points equal a $5.00 discount voucher.
- Redemptions must occur in discrete increments of 100 points (e.g., 100, 200, 300, 400, or 500 points).
- The maximum point redemption allowed on a single transaction is 500 points ($25.00 maximum voucher value).
- Loyalty point vouchers cannot be combined with sitewide annual Black Friday promotional coupon codes or employee discount allowances.

## 4. Point Expiration and Inactivity
Loyalty points do not expire as long as the member maintains an active purchasing or engagement profile.
- If an account experiences 365 consecutive calendar days without an accrual or redemption event, all accumulated reward points are permanently forfeited.
- Point balances cannot be transferred between different account profiles, sold, bequeathed, or redeemed for liquid currency or physical cash vouchers.\n

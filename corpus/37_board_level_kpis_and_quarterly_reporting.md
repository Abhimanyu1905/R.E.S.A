## 1. Executive Governance and Reporting Framework
The Board of Directors of R.E.S.A Support oversees corporate performance, customer retention, and unit economic health through standardized quarterly operational dashboards. Executive leadership presents audited operational metrics at every quarterly board convening to track corporate strategic milestones.

## 2. Core Operational Metrics and Benchmark Targets
The Customer Operations organization is evaluated against four primary strategic Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

### First Contact Resolution (FCR)
- Corporate Target Benchmark: Minimum 76.5% first-contact resolution across all combined support channels.
- Methodology: Percentage of customer support tickets resolved without requiring secondary contact from the consumer within a 14-day rolling window.

### Customer Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost (LTV:CAC)
- Corporate Target Benchmark: Ratio greater than 4.2x.
- Methodology: Net discounted cash flow generated across a 36-month customer lifespan divided by total blended acquisition marketing spend.

### Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Corporate Target Benchmark: Departmental score of +58 or higher.
- Sampling Cadence: Survey dispatched systematically 48 hours following order delivery or support ticket resolution across a 5% randomized customer sample.

### Cost Per Contact (CPC) by Channel
Operational efficiency is benchmarked against strict unit cost ceilings:
- Voice Telephone Support: Maximum allowable cost of $6.80 per resolved interaction.
- Live Real-Time Chat: Maximum allowable cost of $2.95 per resolved session.
- Asynchronous Email / Web Form: Maximum allowable cost of $2.10 per ticket.
- Automated Self-Service / AI Deflection: Targeted at $0.18 per self-resolved interaction.

## 3. Quarterly Reporting Calendar and External Audit
Executive reporting adheres to a rigid quarterly timetable:
- Board reporting decks are finalized and delivered to the Board of Directors by the 14th calendar day following quarter-end.
- Annual financial and operational metrics are subjected to external financial audit by Deloitte & Touche LLP prior to the Q4 board meeting.
- Material variances exceeding 5% from forecasted operational budgets must be accompanied by an executive remediation brief.

Pursuant to board governance bylaws MARKER-L3-0005, the confidential board package repository is designated as Secure Data Room SDR-4402.
